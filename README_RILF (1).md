# RAYYAN INNOVATION AND LEARNING FOUNDATION (RILF)

**Tagline:** Learn Today. Build Tomorrow.

## About RILF

Rayyan Innovation and Learning Foundation (RILF) is a learning-focused initiative committed to making practical education and digital skills more accessible within our communities.

RILF aims to help people build useful skills, develop confidence and prepare for opportunities in an increasingly technology-driven world. Through practical instruction, guided exercises and community-focused learning, the Foundation encourages learners to apply knowledge in education, work and everyday life.

## Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities through accessible practical education, digital-skills training and innovation-focused learning.

## Our Vision

A society where people, especially young people, have the knowledge, digital confidence and practical skills to create opportunities and contribute to community development.

## Our Core Areas

- **Basic Computer Operations:** Introducing learners to computer use, file management, typing, internet browsing and everyday digital tasks.
- **Microsoft Office Training:** Practical learning in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint for education, work and personal productivity.
- **Digital Awareness:** Building understanding of technology and responsible participation in the digital world.
- **Innovation and Problem-Solving:** Encouraging creativity and the use of technology to address real-life challenges.
- **Community Learning:** Supporting a welcoming environment where people can learn, practise and grow together.

## Our Training Approach

RILF prioritises practical, in-person learning. Our approach is built around three steps:

1. **Learn the basics** – Understand essential concepts through clear explanations and guidance.
2. **Practise hands-on** – Apply new knowledge through supervised activities and exercises.
3. **Grow your confidence** – Develop skills that can be used in school, work and everyday life.

Programme dates, duration, fees (where applicable), eligibility and available spaces are announced separately for each training cycle.

## Who We Serve

Our activities are intended for learners and community members who want to improve their computer literacy, strengthen their digital skills or explore innovation. Specific programme requirements may vary.

## Website

The RILF website provides information about the Foundation, its training activities, announcements and contact channels. The activities gallery is intended to feature photographs and videos from actual RILF programmes.

### Website project files

For the current starter website:

- `RILF_Official_Website.html` – the main website file.
- `README.md` – this project and organisation guide.

The website is a standalone HTML page with its styling and JavaScript included. No build tools or installation are required to preview it.

### How to view the website

1. Download `RILF_Official_Website.html`.
2. Locate the file on your computer.
3. Double-click it, or right-click and open it with a web browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Firefox.
4. To make changes, open the file in a code editor such as Visual Studio Code.

### Before publishing

Update the placeholder information in the HTML file:

- Add the Foundation's official logo.
- Add an official email address.
- Add an official phone or WhatsApp number.
- Add the correct training location.
- Replace sample gallery tiles with approved RILF photographs and videos.
- Update programme information, dates and registration instructions.
- Replace the placeholder email recipient in the contact form with the official RILF email address.

**Important:** The current contact form is a front-end email link. It does not store applications or send information to a database. A live registration system, admin dashboard and media-upload feature will require additional backend services or a suitable content-management platform.
