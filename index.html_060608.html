Who we are

Learning that opens doors.

Rayyan Innovation and Learning Foundation (RILF) is a learning-focused initiative committed to making practical education and digital skills more accessible within our communities.

We believe that talent grows when people have access to guidance, hands-on experience and an encouraging environment. Our activities focus on helping learners gain useful skills they can apply in education, work and everyday life.

What we offer

Skills for the real world

Our training is designed around practical participation, guided instruction and skills learners can continue developing beyond the classroom.

🖥️

Basic Computer Operations

Build confidence using a computer, managing files and folders, typing, browsing and carrying out everyday digital tasks.

📄

Microsoft Office

Develop practical skills in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint for school, work, presentations and personal productivity.

💡

Innovation & Digital Awareness

Encourage curiosity, problem-solving and awareness of how technology can be used to address real community needs.
